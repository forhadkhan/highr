About Verdant Grove

Verdant Grove is a neighborhood of 88 townhomes built around shared gardens and car-free lanes. Every home is designed for low running costs: solar-ready roofs, heat-pump heating and cooling, and rainwater harvesting for the gardens.

Homes are naturally ventilated, with windows placed for cross-breezes and deep eaves for summer shade. The community is complete and lived in, with a few move-in ready homes still available.

Highlights

  • Solar-ready roofs and heat-pump heating and cooling
  • Rainwater harvesting for shared gardens
  • Community gardens and car-free lanes
  • Naturally ventilated, shaded layouts
  • EV charging and covered bike storage
  • Move-in ready homes available now
