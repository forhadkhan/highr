About Coastline Residences

Coastline Residences is a small community of 36 villas on the water. Each home has a deep private deck, large sliding glass walls and natural materials — timber, stone and lime plaster — chosen to age well in a coastal climate.

Villas are raised and built to current flood standards. Residents share a sea-view lounge, a private dock and landscaped walking paths that run the length of the site.

Highlights

  • Flood-resilient raised construction
  • Natural timber, stone and lime-plaster finishes
  • Smart-home wiring and EV charging in every garage
  • Landscaped walking paths along the shoreline
