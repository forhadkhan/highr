About us

Homes built to be lived in, and built to last

Highr began in 2014 as a small design-build studio in San Francisco. Today we develop new apartments, townhomes and villas across four US cities, and we stay with every community long after the keys are handed over.

Every Highr home starts with a floor plan we would want to live in: natural light, honest materials and efficient systems as standard. Our architects and builders work under one roof, so what you see on the plans is what you get.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Can’t find your answer? Call (212) 555-0142 or send us a message.

How much do Highr homes cost?

Prices start from $425,000 for apartments and go up to $1.4 million for seaside villas. Each community lists its current “from” price and available home types.

Can I see a home before I buy?

Yes. Show homes are open six days a week at every active community, and we offer live video tours if you are out of town. Book a tour from the contact form below.

How do reservation and financing work?

You reserve a home with a deposit, and your advisor then walks you through the purchase contract, mortgage options and closing timeline. We can introduce you to lenders, but you are free to use your own.

How long does construction take?

Most communities take 14 to 24 months from groundbreaking to handover. Each project card shows how far along it is, and reserved buyers get regular progress updates.

Can I customize finishes?

Yes. Until framing is complete you can choose from curated flooring, kitchen and fixture packages. Structural changes are not available once construction starts.

What warranty comes with my home?

Every home includes a 10-year structural warranty and a 1-year workmanship warranty. Our Customer Care team handles claims and runs a check-in at the 12-month mark.

Do you work with investors?

Yes. Many of our buyers are investors. Ask your advisor about rental-ready finish packages and the expected handover dates for each community.
