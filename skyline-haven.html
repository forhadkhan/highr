About Skyline Haven

Skyline Haven is a 26-story residential tower of studios, one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments. Every home has floor-to-ceiling glass, a private balcony or terrace, and a layout designed around natural light and cross-ventilation.

Shared spaces are built for everyday use: a landscaped podium garden, a rooftop pool, a co-working lounge and a fitness studio. A concierge team is on site around the clock.

Highlights

  • Floor-to-ceiling glass and private terraces

  • Co-working lounge and fitness studio
  • EV charging and secure bike storage
  • 24/7 concierge and package room
  • Smart locks and thermostats in every home
